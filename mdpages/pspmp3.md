## Functions

### [end](#end)
**`end`(...):**  
Stops playback and calls freetune.

### [endofstream](#endofstream)
**`endofstream`(...):**  
Returns 1 if the stream has ended.

### [freetune](#freetune)
**`freetune`(...):**  
Free up used memory and releases MP3 subsystem.

### [gettime](#gettime)
**`gettime`(...):**  
Returns the stream play time in format HH:MM:SS.

### [init](#init)
**`init`(...):**  
Initializes the MP3 subsystem.

### [load](#load)
**`load`(...):**  
Loads a MP3 file.

### [pause](#pause)
**`pause`(...):**  
Pauses MP3 playback, call again to unpause.

### [play](#play)
**`play`(...):**  
Play a loaded MP3 file.

### [setvolume](#setvolume)
**`setvolume`(...):**  
Sets the volume in percentage.

### [stop](#stop)
**`stop`(...):**  
Stop MP3 playback.
